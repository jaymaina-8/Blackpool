Why restaurants in Nairobi need a dedicated website

When someone in Nairobi searches "restaurant near me" or "best café in Westlands," Google shows results based on websites and business listings — not Instagram followers. If your restaurant only has social media, you're missing a large portion of customers who search online before deciding where to eat.

A well-built restaurant website does three things well: it tells customers exactly where you are and what you serve, it makes it easy to contact or order from you, and it builds trust through a professional presentation that social media alone can't match.

Customers who find you through a clear, fast-loading website are more likely to visit — because they've already found the information they needed without friction.

Common questions from restaurant owners

Can customers order food through the website?

Yes. We can integrate a WhatsApp ordering button or a simple order form directly into your site. Customers browse your menu, then tap to send their order via WhatsApp — the simplest and most practical approach for most Nairobi restaurants.

How do I keep the menu updated?

We can set up your site so updates are straightforward, or we can handle menu updates for you as part of ongoing support. Either way, your menu stays accurate without requiring technical knowledge on your part.

Will my restaurant show up on Google?

A website is a strong foundation for Google visibility. We build sites with local SEO in mind. We also strongly recommend setting up a free Google Business Profile alongside your website — this combination significantly improves how you appear in local search results and Google Maps.

Do you work with cafés and food trucks as well?

Yes. We build websites for all kinds of food businesses — restaurants, cafés, food trucks, catering services, and cloud kitchens. The approach is the same: clear menu, easy contact, and good visibility on Google.

How long does it take to launch a restaurant website?

Most restaurant websites go live within 48–72 hours of us receiving your content — menu, photos, and basic business details. If you need help gathering content, we'll guide you through exactly what's needed.
