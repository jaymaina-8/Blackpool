The Project

About Urban Grill

Grill House Restaurant Food Industry Visual Design

Urban Grill is a grill restaurant built for customers who take their food seriously. Grills live and die by visual appeal — the smell, the char, the presentation. A website for this type of business has to translate that experience onto a screen.

Without a strong website, Urban Grill couldn't communicate what made them worth visiting. Customers who hadn't been before had no real way to understand the quality of the food or the experience before showing up.

The Problem

What was missing

Grill restaurants depend heavily on the visual quality of their food to attract customers. Instagram helps, but it's not enough on its own — it doesn't rank in Google, it doesn't show the menu cleanly, and it doesn't give potential customers a professional-feeling place to land when they're making a decision.

No visual showcase

The food quality couldn't be communicated without a dedicated space designed around strong imagery and presentation.

Menu scattered across platforms

The menu lived across Instagram captions and WhatsApp messages — inconsistent and hard to browse for a new customer.

Not searchable on Google

Without a website, Urban Grill was invisible to customers searching for grill restaurants — a significant missed opportunity.

No easy contact path

Customers had to dig through social profiles to find a phone number or DM the business just to ask basic questions.

The Solution

What we built

We designed the Urban Grill website to lead with the food. The visual design uses dark tones, high-contrast imagery, and generous spacing — a look that suits a grill house and makes the photography work harder.

The menu is laid out cleanly by category: starters, mains, grills, sides, and drinks. Each item is presented with enough description to build appetite, without overwhelming the page.

Contact and WhatsApp access are integrated throughout — customers can reach the restaurant immediately, whether they're on the homepage or halfway through the menu.

Visual-first design

The layout and colour choices are built around food photography — high contrast, dark background, bold imagery that communicates quality before a word is read.

Clear menu structure

Menu items organized by category with clear descriptions — easy to browse on mobile, simple to navigate on desktop.

Integrated contact & WhatsApp

Phone, WhatsApp, and location accessible from any page. Customers don't have to search for contact details — they're always visible.

Performance on mobile

Fast-loading design hosted on Vercel, optimized for the mobile browsing conditions most customers in Nairobi experience.

The Result

What changed after launch

Urban Grill now has a website that matches the quality of its food — a professional digital presence that potential customers can land on and immediately understand what the restaurant offers.

  • ✓ Food photography has a proper showcase — visitors get a clear sense of the quality before visiting
  • ✓ The menu is accessible in one tap — no more scattered posts or DMs to find out what's on offer
  • ✓ Customers can reach the restaurant immediately via WhatsApp directly from the menu
  • ✓ A proper URL that can be listed on Google Business Profile, shared on WhatsApp, and printed on menus or packaging
