The Project

About Milestone Creative Solutions

Business Supplies & Equipment Schools & Institutions E-commerce

Milestone Creative Solutions Limited is a Nairobi-based supplier of educational and business resources — serving schools, institutions, and businesses across the city with lab equipment, stationery, textbooks, school furniture, and more.

Their customers — procurement officers, school administrators, and business managers — needed to be able to browse what's available, see prices, and make contact quickly. Without a website, none of that was possible. Every enquiry had to go through a phone call or a referral.

The Problem

What was holding them back

Milestone had an established operation and a real product catalog — but it lived nowhere accessible online. Potential clients (schools, institutions) couldn't verify the business, browse products, or request a quote without making direct contact first.

No online catalog

Customers had no way to browse products, check availability, or compare items before reaching out — creating unnecessary friction in the sales process.

Hard to quote for bulk orders

Schools and institutions buying in bulk had no self-service way to understand pricing or request a formal quote — everything had to be negotiated manually.

No credibility signal online

A school procurement officer searching for suppliers expects to find a professional website. Without one, Milestone lost bids before the conversation even started.

Contact only by phone

Clients had to call to start any conversation. A website with a WhatsApp button and contact form opens that door to far more potential customers.

The Solution

What we built

We built Milestone a professional business website with a full product catalog — structured so potential clients can browse items by category, see prices, and submit a quote request or contact the team directly.

The catalog covers the full range of what Milestone supplies: books and stationery, art and sports equipment, lab equipment, school furniture, and assessments. Each product is listed with a name, image, and price — giving buyers the information they need to qualify the supplier before picking up the phone.

Contact is built for the way Nairobi businesses communicate: WhatsApp button, phone, email, and a contact form with a map for clients who want to visit.

Browsable product catalog

All products listed by category with names, images, and prices — so buyers can self-qualify and shortlist what they need before reaching out.

Quote request system

A dedicated "Get a Quote" flow so institutions and businesses can submit bulk inquiries without a phone call — reducing friction for the most common customer type.

Professional homepage

A clear headline, service description, and call to action — building instant credibility with procurement officers and institutional buyers.

Multi-channel contact

WhatsApp, phone, email, and a contact form with a Google Maps location — covering every way a Nairobi client might want to get in touch.

The Result

What changed after launch

Milestone Creative Solutions now has a professional online presence that does the groundwork before a client ever calls — presenting the full catalog, building credibility, and making it easy to start a purchasing conversation.

  • ✓ Schools and institutions can browse the full product catalog before making contact — reducing cold-call friction
  • ✓ A professional website that signals an established, credible supplier — important when competing for institutional procurement
  • ✓ Clients can request a quote or reach out via WhatsApp directly from the product pages — no phone tag required
  • ✓ A shareable URL to include in procurement tender documents, email signatures, and Google Business Profile
