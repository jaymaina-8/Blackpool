The Project

Making school supply ordering clearer

Education CBC Materials Product Catalog WhatsApp Orders

Topnote Publishers provides revision books, exams, stationery, and lab supplies to families and schools across Kenya. The website needed to feel trustworthy and direct: parents should find the right workbook fast, while schools should quickly understand bulk supply options and start a quote conversation.

We shaped the site around the real buying behavior of the audience. Instead of forcing users through a heavy checkout flow, the experience keeps catalog browsing simple and routes final orders to WhatsApp, where Topnote can confirm availability, delivery, and pricing details.

The Challenge

Two audiences, one smooth path

Parents and schools come to Topnote with different needs. Parents need quick product discovery and confidence in price. Schools need bulk supply clarity, discount messaging, and a low-friction way to request quotes for books, exams, stationery, and lab equipment.

Parent-friendly browsing

Workbook cards needed strong visuals, grade labels, prices in KSh, and simple add-to-cart behavior before WhatsApp checkout.

School supply confidence

The schools page had to explain bulk discounts, nationwide delivery, and fast support without making institutions hunt for details.

Clear contact routes

WhatsApp had to be the primary contact action, backed by an inquiry option for users who prefer a more structured request.

Consistent product trust

The brand needed a clean red-and-white interface that keeps product covers, pricing, and ordering steps easy to scan on mobile.

The Solution

What we delivered

We built a multi-page education commerce website with a direct homepage promise, story-driven about page, product catalog, parent and school pathways, contact page, cart review, and persistent WhatsApp access. The interface keeps Topnote's red brand color prominent while staying quiet enough for products and prices to lead.

  • Homepage positioning for CBC books, exam practice, stationery, and classroom essentials.
  • Product catalog with workbook cards, prices, cart actions, and WhatsApp checkout guidance.
  • Dedicated school page for bulk supply, institution pricing benefits, and delivery support.
  • Contact experience focused on fast WhatsApp replies, nationwide support, and clear pricing.
  • Responsive layouts designed for parents and procurement teams browsing on mobile devices.
The Result

A site designed for real ordering behavior

The final experience gives Topnote a digital storefront that feels practical, credible, and easy to act on. Parents can move from homepage promise to product cards without friction, while schools get a dedicated page that speaks directly to institutional supply needs.

The strongest conversion path is intentionally simple: browse the catalog, review the cart, and continue the conversation on WhatsApp. That keeps the business flexible for stock checks, school discounts, delivery details, and custom quote requests.

"Blackpool helped us turn school supply ordering into a simple online experience. Parents and schools can browse books, understand pricing, and reach us on WhatsApp without friction."
