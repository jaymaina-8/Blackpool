The Project

What Devireen Enterprise needed

B2B Procurement Office Supplies

Devireen Enterprise has grown to become one of Kenya's most reliable B2B suppliers for office supplies, stationery, and equipment.

They needed a streamlined platform that allows their clients—businesses, schools, NGOs, and government institutions—to easily browse their catalogue of 500+ products and request quotes in minutes, establishing trust and reducing manual back-and-forth communication.

The Solution

What we built

We designed a clean, professional B2B website focused on product discovery and procurement efficiency.

Clear Product Catalogue

Over 500 products neatly organized by categories, making it easy for procurement teams to find exactly what they need.

Fast Quote Requests

Integrated an intuitive "Request Quote" and "Add to Cart" system, streamlining the procurement process for large orders.

Direct WhatsApp Integration

Added a floating WhatsApp chat button, allowing clients to get instant support and quick answers to their queries.

Trust & Authority

Dedicated sections highlighting their nationwide delivery, 24-hr quote turnaround, and 6+ years of experience to build immediate trust.
